02 — Two worlds, one path

A boy from the foothills
building the future of data by the sea.

My story crosses two continents and one shared obsession: turning messy, real-world information into things people can actually use. Here is how the two halves of that story fit together.

नेपाल · Nepal

Origin · 27.71° N, 85.32° E

I grew up between the foothills of the Himalayas and the buzz of Kathmandu — a place where prayer flags ripple over satellite dishes and your morning chai is served beside a 5G signal. That dual rhythm of old craft and new tech is where my curiosity for engineering started.

I went on to earn a Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Science & Engineering at Lovely Professional University, India — laying down foundations in algorithms, databases, networks and systems thinking.

THE BRIDGE — TECHNOLOGY

Australia · 🇦🇺

Current chapter · 27.13° S, 152.95° E

Today I'm a Master of ICT student at the University of the Sunshine Coast, just inland from the QLD coast — where I traded snow caps for surf breaks. My capstone with Sunshine Coast Council Libraries shipped a real Power BI reporting solution to a real community.

Alongside study I work in regulated aged care — high-trust, audited, documentation-heavy. The discipline that demands maps directly onto the rigour real data work needs.

“Between the Annapurnas and the Coral Sea sits the same human question — how do we make sense of what we measure? That is the question I want to keep answering, with code, clarity and care.”
